    How it works

    Script on your computer, camera on your phone. Open this page on the computer, paste the script, tap ⋯ then Send to phone, and scan the code with your phone's camera.

    Script cues. [pause] and (smile) show as cues, not words to read. *word* adds emphasis. A line of --- splits one paste into several scripts.

    Getting the video back. After a take, tap Share to send it by AirDrop, Google Drive, iCloud or WhatsApp, or straight into CapCut. On a computer it saves to Downloads.

    Recording on a computer. This page works with a laptop webcam too. Space pauses, R records, Page Up redoes a line.

    Make it an app. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. In Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app.
