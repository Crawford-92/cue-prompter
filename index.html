      Use with Claude

      Add the Cue Prompter skill to your Claude once. After that, every finished script ends with a link. Tap it on your phone and the script lands here. On a computer, the link shows a code to scan with your phone.

      Download the Claude skill

      In Claude, open Settings, find Skills, and upload the file. Your scripts stay private: they travel inside the link and are only saved on your phone.

      How it works

      Film. Tap Film this. The script scrolls under the camera as you speak. Tap the text to rehearse first.

      Fluffed a line? Tap Redo line and say the sentence again. The mistake is cut from the clean video.

      Finish. Make the clean video, then share it to CapCut, TikTok, AirDrop or Photos.
